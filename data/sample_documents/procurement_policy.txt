# SupplyWise AI — Sample Procurement Policy

Document ID: POL-001
Version: 1.0

## 1. Supplier Delivery Requirements

Suppliers must deliver standard purchase orders within 7 calendar days of order confirmation unless a different lead time is agreed in writing.

## 2. Late Delivery Penalties

If a supplier delivers an order more than 3 calendar days after the agreed delivery date, the procurement team must review the delay and document the reason.

For delays exceeding 7 calendar days beyond the agreed delivery date, the buyer may apply a penalty of 2% of the delayed order value, subject to the signed supplier contract.

## 3. Minimum Order Quantity

The purchase order quantity must meet the supplier's agreed minimum order quantity (MOQ). Any exception requires written approval from the procurement manager.

## 4. Supplier Reliability

Supplier reliability must be evaluated using documented delivery performance and quality records. Price alone must not determine supplier selection.

## 5. Approval Requirements

Purchase orders exceeding ₹100,000 require approval from the procurement manager before the order is issued.
